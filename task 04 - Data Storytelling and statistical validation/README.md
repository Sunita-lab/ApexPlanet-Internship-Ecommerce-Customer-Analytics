# 🧪 Statistical Validation

### Null Hypothesis (H₀)

There is no statistically significant difference in the average sales of male and female customers.

### Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)

There is a statistically significant difference in the average sales of male and female customers.

### Test Used

Independent Two-Sample T-Test

### Results

* **T-statistic:** 0.6826
* **P-value:** 0.4950
* **Significance Level (α):** 0.05

### Decision

Fail to Reject the Null Hypothesis (H₀)

### Interpretation

The statistical analysis indicates that the difference in average sales between male and female customers is **not statistically significant**. This suggests that gender alone is not a strong factor influencing customer spending behaviour in this dataset.
